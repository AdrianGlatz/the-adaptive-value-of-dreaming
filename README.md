# The Adaptive Value of Dreaming

## Project Description

In this project, we explore the extent to which dreaming, conceptualized as a form of offline learning, may provide an evolutionary advantage.

To investigate this question, we develop an artificial evolutionary environment in which simulated organisms compete for survival and reproduction. 
By comparing populations with and without dream-based learning under controlled conditions, we aim to determine whether dreaming improves predictive capabilities, adaptability, and ultimately long-term evolutionary success.

## Research Question

To what extent does dreaming, conceptualized as a form of offline learning, enhance an organism's predictive capabilities and thereby contribute to its evolutionary success?
